# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Take snapshots of a sandbox on its card, and delete them again.
- Restore a snapshot into a new sandbox. You can choose a new network and new folders for it.
- Deleting a sandbox also deletes its snapshots.
- Upload files from your computer into a running sandbox.
